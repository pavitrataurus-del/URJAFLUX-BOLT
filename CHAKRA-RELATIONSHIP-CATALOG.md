## Overview & Relationship Types

The **Enterprise Chakra Intelligence Library** defines 14 explicit relationship types to support multi-dimensional knowledge graph traversal and future semantic reasoning:

1. `SUPPORTS`: Source entity strengthens/underpins target entity.
2. `BALANCES`: Source entity harmonizes or counteracts excess in target entity.
3. `WEAKENS`: Source entity dampens target entity function when uncalibrated.
4. `BLOCKS`: Source entity restricts or obstructs subtle prana flow in target entity.
5. `STRENGTHENS`: Source entity reinforces target entity capacity.
6. `AFFECTS`: Direct operational influence between entities.
7. `CONNECTED_TO`: Structural or anatomical connection.
8. `LOCATED_IN`: Anatomical or spatial mapping.
9. `ASSOCIATED_WITH`: Qualitative or symbolic correspondence.
10. `INTERACTS_WITH`: Dynamic reciprocal interplay.
11. `DEPENDS_ON`: Hierarchical prerequisite requirement.
12. `CONFLICTS_WITH`: Contradictory relationship.
13. `REMEDIED_BY`: Therapeutic intervention mapping.
14. `INFLUENCES`: Downstream behavioral effect.
