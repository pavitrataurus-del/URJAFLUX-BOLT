# Knowledge Confidence Engine Report

## Confidence Grade Scale
- **Grade A+** ($95 - 100\%$): Unanimous primary shastra backing, zero active conflicts, multi-domain alignment.
- **Grade A** ($85 - 94\%$): Strong primary shastra backing with SME approval.
- **Grade B** ($70 - 84\%$): Moderate evidence with secondary supporting commentary.
- **Grade C** ($55 - 69\%$): Partial evidence under SME review.
- **Grade F** ($< 55\%$): Unverified or heavily disputed claims.

## Auto-Update Behavior
Confidence scores and grades automatically re-calculate whenever new evidence sources are attached or expert votes are cast.
