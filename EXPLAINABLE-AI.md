# Explainable AI & Rule Traceability Specification — DOMAIN-006

## Philosophy
Black-box recommendations are prohibited in URJAFLUX AI OS. Every output generated by DOMAIN-006 produces an auditable `IReasoningChain`.

## Reasoning Chain Attributes
- **`chainId`**: Unique identifier for audit logging.
- **`recommendationId`**: Link to generated recommendation.
- **`steps`**: Array of `IReasoningStep` items outlining:
  - Step Number & Title
  - Input Nodes Used
  - Rule Applied
  - Confidence Weight Contribution
- **`ruleHierarchy`**:
  - Tier 1: Canonical Shastra Texts (Priority Score: 100)
  - Tier 2: Historical Commentaries & Gutke Manuscripts (Priority Score: 88)
  - Tier 3: Modern Architectural & Contextual Adaptations (Priority Score: 75)
- **`rejectedEvidence`**: List of excluded items with exact rejection reasons (e.g. OCR quality <80%).
- **`explanationSummary`**: Natural language summary describing contributing domains, source citations, and confidence grade.
