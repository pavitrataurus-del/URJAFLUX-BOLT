# Knowledge Evidence Engine Report

## Overview
The Evidence Engine attaches primary, supporting, contradicting, research, expert, and historical references to every extracted rule.

## Tracked Attributes
- **Primary Sources**: Classical Sanskrit shastras (e.g., Mayamatam, Manasara, Samarangana Sutradhara).
- **Supporting Sources**: Modern commentary and academic translations.
- **Contradicting Sources**: Variant manuscripts or cross-tradition divergences.
- **Research Sources**: Peer-reviewed geopathic and architectural studies.
- **Expert References**: Certified Acharyas and Vaidya SMEs.
- **Historical References**: Epigraphic and archaeological site evidence.
- **Metrics**: Evidence Count, Evidence Strength (0-100), Evidence Quality (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW), Evidence Freshness.
