# Decision Intelligence Engine (DECISION-INTELLIGENCE.md)

## 1. Description
The Decision Intelligence Engine translates quantitative measures into qualitative, actionable risk and opportunity indicators.

## 2. Capabilities
- **Risk Indicators:** Pinpoint upcoming SLA and workload bottlenecks.
- **Opportunity Indicators:** Highlight potential structural performance gains.
- **Cost Indicators:** Surface under-utilized server capacity to trim overhead.
- **Confidence Scores:** Every insight generates an algorithmic confidence rating (e.g. 91% confidence) based on historical evidence.
