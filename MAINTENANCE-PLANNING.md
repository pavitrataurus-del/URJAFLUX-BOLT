# Maintenance Planning Specification — DOMAIN-008

## Overview
`MaintenancePlanningService.ts` manages preventive, corrective, routine, and scheduled maintenance for installed remedies across property digital twins.

## Maintenance Types
- **Preventive Maintenance**: Scheduled cleaning, crystal re-energization, or copper polishing.
- **Corrective Maintenance**: Fixing damaged or displaced physical anchors.
- **Routine Maintenance**: Periodic spirit-level and compass orientation checks.
- **Scheduled Maintenance**: Milestone-driven environmental recalibration.

## Data Attributes
- Assigned Engineer & Role
- Scheduled Date vs Completed Date
- Estimated Cost Placeholder (No accounting logic)
- Inspector Notes & Alignment Guidelines
