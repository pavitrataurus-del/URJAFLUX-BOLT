# Checklist Engine Specification — DOMAIN-007

## Overview
The Checklist Engine provides reusable verification templates and task-level execution checklists.

## Verification Requirements
Individual checklist items can require:
- **Photo Evidence**: Field image capture
- **Video Recording**: Operational video log
- **Document Attachment**: Test certificate or PDF
- **Digital Signature**: Client or inspector sign-off
- **GPS Coordinates**: Geolocation tagging
- **Physical Measurement**: Directional angles, dimensions, or frequencies
- **Notes**: Inspector remarks
