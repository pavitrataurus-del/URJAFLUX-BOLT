# DOMAIN-010 — Dynamic Section Builder

## Overview
The **Dynamic Section Builder** (`/src/core/reports/ReportDataOrchestrator.ts`) constructs modular report sections based on report type and available domain data.

## Supported Dynamic Sections
- **Cover Page**
- **Executive Summary**
- **Property & Spatial Specifications**
- **Project Execution & Scope**
- **Knowledge Findings & Scriptural References**
- **Multi-Domain Energy Reasoning**
- **Prioritized Recommendations**
- **Site Remediation Execution Status**
- **Digital Twin & IoT Telemetry**
- **Sthapatya Veda Compliance Rating**
- **Evidence & Photo Logs**
- **Appendices & Citation Vault**

## Reordering & Visibility
Sections can be toggled visible/hidden, reordered, or edited live inside the **Report Builder Workspace**.
