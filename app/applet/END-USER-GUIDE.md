# ENTERPRISE END-USER GUIDE
**URJAFLUX AI OS — Version BUILD-026I (RC-1)**

---

## 1. Overview
Welcome to URJAFLUX AI OS. This portal provides intuitive access to spatial analytics, digital twins, vastu compliance summaries, and executive deliverables.

## 2. Navigating the Workspace

### Project Explorer & Digital Twin
- Select your active building project from the top dropdown or project list.
- View interactive 2D floor plans and 3D Digital Twin layouts.
- Click on rooms or zones to view localized properties and environmental compliance metrics.

### Viewing Vastu Analysis & Recommendations
- Access the **Vastu Workspace** tab to inspect compliance ratings.
- Review remedies, priority fixes, and spatial adjustments calculated for each zone.
- Access high-level explanations without complex technical jargon.

### Downloading Reports
- Navigate to **Report Center**.
- Access published executive summaries and compliance certificates.
- Click **PDF** or **DOCX** to download approved client deliverables.
