- **All Public APIs:** `IngestionApi`, `OcrApi`, `SpatialApi`, `EmbeddingApi`, `DigitalTwinApi`, `GraphApi`, `ReasoningApi`, `DecisionApi`
- **Internal APIs:** `OntologyEngine`, `VectorStore`, `RecommendationEngine`
- **Repository APIs:** `TwinRepository`, `GraphRepository`, `DecisionRepository`, `RecommendationRepository`
- **Factory APIs:** `EmbeddingProviderFactory`, `VectorStoreFactory`, `TwinRepositoryFactory`, `GraphRepositoryFactory`, `DecisionRepositoryFactory`
- **Validation APIs:** `DecisionValidator`, `ExpertValidator`, `SpatialValidator`
- **Reasoning APIs:** `AIReasoningEngine`, `ExpertExecutionEngine`
- **Twin APIs:** `DigitalTwinApi`, `TwinObjectManager`
- **Knowledge Graph APIs:** `GraphApi`, `KnowledgeGraphHydrator`
