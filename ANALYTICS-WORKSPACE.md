# Analytics Workspace & Administration Console (ANALYTICS-WORKSPACE.md)

## 1. Description
The Analytics Workspace is the unified console for admins and data teams to manage URJAFLUX operational telemetry.

## 2. Workspace Tabs
1. **Executive Dashboard:** Visual charts presenting cross-domain statuses.
2. **Dashboard Builder:** Configures titles, charts, and grid parameters.
3. **KPI Explorer:** Formulary to specify new configurable metrics.
4. **Trend Explorer:** Plots historical sequences with anomaly markers.
5. **Forecast Center:** Standard area projections with confidence intervals.
6. **Dataset Explorer:** Queries underlying raw time-series logs.
7. **Decision Insights:** Curated action cards mapped to evidence values.
8. **Administration:** Policy definitions for partition retention and role visibilities.
